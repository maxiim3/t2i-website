# Estimation Guidelines

## Core Rule: Story Points Only

**DO NOT** use time-based estimates:
- ❌ No hours
- ❌ No days  
- ❌ No weeks
- ❌ No "2-week sprints"

**DO** use story points:
- ✅ Story points (1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13 scale)
- ✅ Velocity targets in points
- ✅ Sprint capacity in points

## Story Point Scale

- **1 point**: Simple task, well understood
- **2 points**: Straightforward with minor complexity
- **3 points**: Standard feature, some unknowns
- **5 points**: Complex feature or multiple components
- **8 points**: Large feature with dependencies
- **13 points**: Epic-level, consider breaking down

## Why Points Not Time

1. **Focus on complexity**, not duration
2. **Avoid false precision** - development is unpredictable
3. **Team velocity** emerges naturally over sprints
4. **Less pressure** - no artificial deadlines
5. **Better planning** - relative sizing is more accurate

## Sprint Planning

- Define sprint by **point capacity**, not duration
- Track **velocity** as points completed per sprint
- Adjust future sprints based on **actual velocity**
- Success measured by **points delivered**, not time taken
